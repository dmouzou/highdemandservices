FAQ

Quick answers

Is it really free for homeowners?

Yes. We do not charge homeowners. The business that does your job charges for its work, and you agree the price with them before work starts.

Do you do the work?

No. HighServiceDemand is a matching service, not a contractor. The work is done by independent local businesses, which are responsible for their own work, licenses, insurance and warranties.

How fast will someone call me?

We aim to have a pro call you back quickly. Response times vary by service, area and time of day. For urgent problems, call (000) 000-0000.

What does a job cost?

Prices are set by the pro and depend on the job. Ask for a written quote before work starts. Asking for a quote does not oblige you to hire.
