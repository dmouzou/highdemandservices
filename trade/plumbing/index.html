Why plumbing businesses need a steady flow of calls

Plumbing demand is urgent and local. A homeowner with a burst pipe calls the first plumber who answers and rarely calls a third. If your phone is not ringing at that moment, the job goes to someone else. Steady lead generation keeps the calendar full between emergencies and turns slow weeks into water heater and repipe quotes.
