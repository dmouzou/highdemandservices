The process

Six steps, explained

Here is exactly what happens, who does what and how long it takes.

1. A 15 minute conversation

Use the form, call us or book a meeting. We ask about your trade, the services you want more of, your service area, your hours and how many jobs you can take on each week. We also ask what a good job is worth to you so we know which calls matter.

Nothing is sold on this call. If we are not a fit, we say so.

2. We map your territory and offer

We confirm the cities or ZIP codes you want, the radius you can serve and the types of work to include or exclude. Tell us about jobs you do not want, such as small repairs or commercial work, and we filter them out.

  • Service area by city, ZIP or radius
  • Services to include and exclude
  • Call hours and the numbers calls should ring

3. We build and launch your campaign

We create the marketing that gets homeowners in your area to pick up the phone, tuned to your trade. We handle the creative, the targeting and the tracking. You do not need to learn ad platforms or manage budgets.

Most clients see their first calls within days of launch, depending on trade and area. We confirm timing on your call.

4. Every call is screened

Before a call reaches you, we check that the caller is a real person, is asking for your trade, is inside your service area and is not a duplicate. Calls that fail our automatic rules are credited, and anything else can be disputed.

  • Real person, not a bot or a robocall
  • Needs your trade, not a different one
  • Located inside your service area
  • Not a repeat of a call you already received

5. Calls ring your phone, and only yours

Qualified calls are delivered live to the number you choose, with the caller's request logged. You answer, ask the four key questions (address, problem, urgency, best number) and offer a time. Our clients book about 73% of their calls into appointments.

Every call is exclusive. It is never resold and never goes to another business.

6. Billing, credits and reporting

Pay-per-call accounts get a weekly invoice by email, with a call-by-call breakdown, charged to the card on file. Monthly plans are billed upfront each month, and calls above plan volume appear on the next invoice. You are billed for valid calls only.

Calls that break our rules are credited automatically. Anything else can be disputed within 72 hours and is reviewed and credited within 2 business days. Every week we email a report with calls, sources, outcomes and credits, and the call log.

Want to change volume, pause or stop? Give 7 days notice. That is the whole contract.

Exclusivity

How we keep calls exclusive

Each call is routed to exactly one business in your area and trade. We do not sell, share or resell calls. If two clients in the same trade overlap on territory, we adjust the territories so each call still has one owner.

You can ask for this in writing. We will put it in the terms we confirm before you start.

Process questions

How fast will I get my first calls?

Timing depends on your trade and area. Many clients see calls within days of launch. We confirm it on your call.

Can I choose which jobs I get?

Yes. Tell us what to include and exclude and we filter by service type.

Do I need to manage ads?

No. We run the marketing. You just answer the phone.

What if I get too many calls?

Tell us and we lower volume. You can also pause with 7 days notice.

How do I see what I paid for?

We email a weekly report with each call, its source, its outcome and any credits. The call log is attached.

How does billing work?

Pay-per-call accounts get a weekly invoice by email, charged to the card on file. Monthly plans are billed upfront each month.

What if I get an invalid call?

Calls under 30 seconds, outside your area or repeated within 30 days are credited automatically. Others can be disputed within 72 hours and are reviewed in 2 business days.
